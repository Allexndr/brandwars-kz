# 47 — Law on Advertising (Kazakhstan)
- URL: https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/Z030000508_
- Accessed: 2026-08-27
- Source type: primary (statute)
- Credibility (1-5): 5 (Official legislation)
- Recency: Core law from 2003, amendments through 2025
- Bias notes: None

## Verbatim quotes
> "реклама должна быть достоверной и распознаваемой" (Advertising must be truthful and immediately recognizable)
> "Запрещается реклама...этилового спирта, табака, интернет-казино, незарегистрированных ЛП, незарегистрированных РО" (Prohibited: alcohol, tobacco, online casinos, unregistered medicines/religious orgs)

## Relevance
Critical restrictions for this platform's ToS and content moderation: (1) All advertising must be truthful and recognizable. (2) Unfair advertising is prohibited (including false comparisons, hidden terms, misleading claims). (3) Claims must be supported by evidence on request. (4) Cannot conceal essential contract terms in ads. For paid rankings: if the ranking reflects payment (not merit), this should not be misrepresented as "best" or "recommended" without disclosure.
