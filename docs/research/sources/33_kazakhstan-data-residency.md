# 33 — Kazakhstan Data Residency & Privacy Requirements

## Verbatim quotes

> "Any database containing the personal data of Kazakhstan citizens must be physically located on servers within the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan"
> "a foreign cloud provider's infrastructure — even if accessible from Kazakhstan — does not satisfy this requirement unless the server is physically in Kazakhstan"
> "entities found non-compliant receive mandatory prescriptions requiring remediation"

## Relevance

Critical blocker: Personal data (names, IINs, transaction records, merchant info) must reside on physically Kazakhstan-based servers. Applies to all orgs processing KZ residents' data, including foreign companies without KZ legal entity. Cross-border transfer only permitted with specific consent, interstate agreements, or MCRIAP notification. Recent amendments (August 25, 2026) introduced criminal liability for officers in negligent data leaks.
