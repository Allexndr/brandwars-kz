## Verbatim quotes

> "Pure novelty sites depending entirely on humor or absurdity generate intense but brief traffic spikes, and without sustainable value proposition, traffic decays exponentially after the initial viral period."

> "Single-source monetization is now the fastest way to lose a business overnight, and the most resilient sites stack 3–5 revenue streams."

> "Viral novelty tends to fade quickly, and similar boards typically experience rapid decay in engagement."

## Relevance

Answers Research Question 1 on clone/novelty site survival. Critical finding: novelty sites (including pay-to-rank leaderboards) experience exponential traffic decay once the viral period ends. Multi-source revenue is necessary for sustainability. Application to topbrands.kz model: initial viral surge ($21k in 24 hours for Outbid.lol) does not predict long-term revenue. Without additional revenue streams or ongoing novelty/mechanic improvements, the platform risks sharp decay after 2-4 weeks.
