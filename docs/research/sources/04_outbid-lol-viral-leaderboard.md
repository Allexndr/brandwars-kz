## Verbatim quotes

> "German developer Jonathan Wilke created Outbid.lol, a pay-to-rank leaderboard where users bid money to claim the top spot. Built in three hours using AI coding tools, it launched on X on August 20, 2026."

> "Founder-reported metrics: 1.1 million visitors in two days, $21,000 in first 24 hours, top bid exceeded $13,000. Wilke reportedly declined a $100,000 acquisition offer."

> "The real story isn't the auction mechanic—it's distribution. Dozens of copycats launched within 48 hours but captured minimal traffic because they lacked Wilke's existing audience. The mechanic was never the moat. The distribution was."

> "A trivial-to-build feature combined with an established social following outperformed months of traditional product development. Viral novelty tends to fade quickly, and similar boards typically experience rapid decay in engagement."

## Relevance

Answers Research Question 2 on pay-to-rank sites and Research Question 3 on PvP/competitive mechanics. Outbid.lol is a direct precedent for topbrands.kz model: simple leaderboard, bidding to hold #1, viral spread. Key insight: mechanic is trivial; success depends on existing audience. Caution: success was 24-48 hours old at reporting; long-term sustainability unknown. $21k/day run rate would annualize to ~$7.6M if sustained, but "rapid decay" is expected.
