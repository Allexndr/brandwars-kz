## Relevance

Kaspi is not a competitor but an essential integration point. With 70% of e-commerce and 14M monthly users (74% of population), Kaspi represents the dominant channel for reaching businesses and consumers. Retail media advertising is growing 64% YoY with only 13% merchant penetration—opportunity to position "battle for rank" concept as complementary to Kaspi's retail media.
