## Verbatim quotes

> "Kazakhstan's online retail market reached 1.7 trillion tenge in H1 2025, growing 19% year-over-year. The sector now represents 17.1% of total retail sales"
> "Marketplaces control the landscape, accounting for 92% of online sales and 96% of all transactions"
> "The advertising market reached 60.2 billion tenge, increasing roughly 18% year-over-year"
> "Digital advertising dominates at 53% of all advertising investment. Within digital channels, social media leads at approximately 40% of budgets, followed by search advertising at 15.8%"
> "The number of transactions grew by 27% while average order values declined 6%, indicating consumers are shopping online more frequently for everyday purchases"

## Relevance

Marketplaces dominate (92% of online sales) but only represent portion of local services market. Rising transaction volume with falling order values suggests SMBs in services/local categories (restaurants, repairs, salons) are NOT captured in e-commerce data. Social media advertising is 40% of digital budgets—indicates significant spend on brand/awareness building, not just performance. This suggests "battle for rank" can capture spending from both categories and FMCG/retail.
