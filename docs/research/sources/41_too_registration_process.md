## Relevance
Primary guide for ТОО registration in Kazakhstan. Confirms: 19-step process, online via egov.kz, minimum requirements include IIN/ЭЦП for founders, charter capital varies by business type (minimum 100 МРП for most entities, 0 for small business), choice of tax regime at registration. Registration consolidates bank account opening and insurance into one procedure.
