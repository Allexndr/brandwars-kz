# 30 — Mobile Internet & Connectivity in Kazakhstan 2025

- Source type: primary research
- Credibility (1-5): 5 (DataReportal is authoritative global digital research)

## Verbatim quotes

> "19.2 million individuals using the internet in Kazakhstan at the start of 2025, when online penetration stood at 92.9 percent"
> "26.6 million cellular mobile connections were active in Kazakhstan in early 2025 (128 percent of the total population)"
> "56% of web traffic in Kazakhstan comes from mobile phones"
> "The median mobile internet download speed in Kazakhstan increased by 18.20 Mbps (+51.3 percent) in twelve months to January 2025"

## Relevance

Mobile is non-negotiable for Kazakhstan market: 92.9% internet penetration, 128% mobile connections (multiple devices per person), 56% of web traffic mobile. Combined with earlier finding that 86% of ad traffic is mobile, platform must be mobile-native app or responsive web-first. Internet speed improvements (+51% YoY) mean video/media features are feasible. High penetration + improving infrastructure supports nationwide launch ambition.
