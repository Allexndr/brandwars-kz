## Relevance

Compares Freedom Pay, Epay, CloudPayments, Woopay, Jusan Tole. Epay: bank-backed, local optimization. CloudPayments (now TipTopPay): multicurrency, higher commissions. Freedom Pay: strong security, higher costs. Fees 1-15% depending on provider and transaction type. Recommends evaluating against specific business requirements and transaction volume.
