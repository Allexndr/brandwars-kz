## Verbatim quotes

> "Philadelphia man Nick Greenawalt turned a joke about unemployment into a viral business by selling ad space on his toilet. The animator launched the 'Million Dollar Toilet' challenge on February 2, offering tiers from $1,500 basic spots to $75,000 first-class thrones."

> "The first video featured an ad for Microsoft Teams, and it absolutely exploded. We're talking tens of millions of views, with one clip soaring past 40 million. Brands like MoonPay, Doodles, and Dude Wipes jumped in, pulling in $17,000 in the first week."

> "Nick says brands love it because you can't skip it. You're locked in."

> "His quirky idea attracted major brands like Clorox, MoonPay, and Dr Squatch, generating millions of views online."

## Relevance

Answers Research Question 2 on novelty donation-counter/pay-for-rank sites. Shows modern revival of novelty model with live 3D object + advertising (not just pixels). Data point: $17k in first week with multiple brand bidding suggests competitive spend is working, but launched only Feb 2026—lifespan unknown. Demonstrates the model can still go viral (40M+ views) but sustainability unproven.
