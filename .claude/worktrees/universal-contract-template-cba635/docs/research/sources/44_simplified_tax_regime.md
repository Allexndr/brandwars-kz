## Relevance
For a paid-ranking advertising platform: simplified regime is primarily for B2C retail, not B2B services. A platform taking commissions/payments for rankings would likely NOT qualify for simplified regime. Critical deadline: February 28, 2026 to notify if staying on simplified regime from 2025; automatic transfer to general tax system (ОУР) if no notification.
