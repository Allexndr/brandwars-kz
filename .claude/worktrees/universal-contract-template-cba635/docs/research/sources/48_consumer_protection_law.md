## Verbatim quotes
> "Потребитель имеет право на получение полной, надежной и своевременной информации" (Consumer has right to complete, reliable, timely information)
> "Доступность и надежность информации о товарах, работах и услугах" (Accessibility and reliability of information about goods, works, services)

## Relevance
Foundational consumer protection: (1) Consumers have explicit right to complete, reliable information about goods/services and their providers. (2) Platform operators must ensure consumers are informed. (3) For paid rankings: if end-users visiting the platform are viewing a leaderboard, they have a consumer right to know that ranking reflects payment, not quality/merit. Failure to disclose this could constitute misleading practice. Platform's public-facing disclaimer should state: "Ranking positions are paid placements. Placement does not reflect business quality or customer satisfaction."
