## Verbatim quotes

> "Peer-to-peer gamification integrates game mechanics into fundraising to increase engagement. When participants reach milestones, their brains release dopamine—the same reward chemical triggered by video games. This makes fundraising feel as satisfying as gaming itself."

> "Leaderboards: Public displays of top fundraisers provide visible recognition. Getting public recognition for our achievements is very rewarding, making leaderboards particularly powerful motivators."

> "When donors see their name on a ranked list, they're more likely to give again—and give more. As pledges come in, donors see their name climb the ranks and the room's energy builds. That instant recognition creates a feedback loop: other guests see the board updating, feel the momentum, and reach for their wallets."

> "A study by the National Bureau of Economic Research found that making donation amounts visible significantly increased both the frequency and size of contributions."

## Relevance

Answers Research Question 3 on competitive-spend mechanics with real data. NBER study documents: visibility of rank + live leaderboard updates + public name display = measurably increased donation frequency and size. This is empirical proof that leaderboard visibility drives spend. Directly applicable to the "attack/defend" model: when a business sees their rank drop, the feedback loop incentivizes re-spending to reclaim position.
