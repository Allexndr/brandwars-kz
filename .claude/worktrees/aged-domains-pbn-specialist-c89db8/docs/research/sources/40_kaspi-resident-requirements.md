## Relevance

Kaspi Pay merchant onboarding: Open business account with Kaspi Bank → connect Kaspi Pay → demonstrate 2 months activity. Non-residents (foreign entities) must provide trade register extract. Kazakhstan residents (likely your target) need BIN (legal entity) or IIN (individual). No explicit prohibition on foreign entity ownership/control; consult Kaspi compliance team. Fastest path: register KZ LLC, open Kaspi business account.
