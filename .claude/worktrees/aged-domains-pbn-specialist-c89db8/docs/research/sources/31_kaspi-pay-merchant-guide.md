## Relevance

Documents official Kaspi Pay onboarding process for merchants. Requires settlement account, registration certificate (BIN for legal entities, IIN for sole proprietors), and optionally power of attorney. Fees vary by transaction type and seller turnover; exact rates appear in merchant agreements. Payouts follow merchant's tariff plan (daily, weekly, or custom).
