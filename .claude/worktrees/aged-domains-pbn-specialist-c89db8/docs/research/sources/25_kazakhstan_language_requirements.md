# 25 — Kazakhstan Language Requirements for Commercial Platforms

- URL: https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/kazakhstan-labelingmarking-requirements
- Accessed: 2026-08-27
- Source type: government/official
- Credibility (1-5): 5 (U.S. State Department trade guide citing Kazakh law)
- Recency: 2026
- Bias notes: None observed

## Verbatim quotes

> "For commercial platforms and advertising, advertising must be presented in Kazakh language and is commonly expected in Kazakh and Russian languages"
> "According to Kazakh legislation, most imported products must be labeled in both Kazakh and Russian"
> "Kazakh and Russian are official languages for commercial purposes. Additionally, advertisements in printed publications, on the Internet and those used by news agencies must be in both official languages"
> "For business registration, a company name must be duplicated in the Kazakh language"

## Relevance

**HARD REQUIREMENT:** Platform must support bilingual (Kazakh/Russian) content from launch. This is not optional per Kazakh law for commercial platforms. However, dominant-language targeting (Russian for initial launch) may be acceptable during pilot phase if full Kazakh localization follows within defined timeline.
