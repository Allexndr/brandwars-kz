## Relevance
Covers 9-step ИП registration process: obtain IIN, residency permit if needed, digital signature (ЭЦП), choose tax regime, select ОКЭД codes, register online, open business account, obtain cash register/POS terminal as needed. Key distinction: ИП are eligible for certain simplified tax regimes with no income tax requirement.
