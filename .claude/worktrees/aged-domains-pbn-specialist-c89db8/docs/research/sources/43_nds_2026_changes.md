## Relevance
Critical for tax planning: VAT registration is mandatory at 43.25 million tenge annual revenue (10,000 МРП). Standard rate is 16% (effective 2026). Registration deadline shortened to 5 business days from exceeding threshold. Key point: simplified regime (СНР) provides VAT exemption for eligible businesses, but 2.56 billion tenge annual revenue limit (600,000 МРП) applies.
