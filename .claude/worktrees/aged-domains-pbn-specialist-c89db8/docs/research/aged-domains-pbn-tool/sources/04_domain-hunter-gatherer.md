## Relevance

This is the single strongest piece of evidence for **H1** found in this workstream, precisely because it comes from a vendor's own product-marketing copy rather than a skeptical reviewer or forum: Domain Hunter Gatherer's official page states outright that "Metrics point you at candidates. They do not make the decision for you," and that "deciding which names deserve your money is still a human job," explicitly instructing users to manually "check trademarks, read the archived pages, look at where the links actually come from." A vendor voluntarily admitting its own automation stops short of the final judgment call — rather than claiming full automation — is strong, low-bias confirmation that the market's existing self-serve tools (including this one) are built and marketed as triage/candidate-surfacing layers, consistent with the report's H4 (automation ceiling) as well as H1. No mention of spam/casino/adult/pharma content-category detection was found on this page; its differentiators are keyword-based and outbound-link-crawling discovery methods, not red-flag screening.
