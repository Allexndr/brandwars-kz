## Relevance

This is the strongest evidence against H2's pessimism found in this workstream. At roughly $0.06 per 1,000 backlink rows and $0.024 per call, pulling a backlink summary for a candidate domain costs a small fraction of a dollar — vetting "hundreds of candidate domains monthly" plausibly costs single-digit-to-low-double-digit dollars per month, versus Ahrefs/Majestic's $130–$400+/month fixed subscriptions. This is direct support for the "thin wrapper over rented data" framing in the plan's opposition queries: a solo builder can get backlink-adjacent data affordably on a true pay-as-you-go basis with (per other coverage found this session) no fixed monthly commitment as of mid-2026. The open caveat is metric trust — DataForSEO's own backlink metrics are not "Ahrefs DR" or "Majestic TF/CF," and buyers in this niche job post specifically named Ahrefs and Majestic, so cost feasibility here doesn't fully resolve whether the output would be trusted by the same buyers.
