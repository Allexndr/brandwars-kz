# 20 — Niche Pursuits: "Alright Google, You Win...I'll Never Use Private Blog Networks Again!"

- URL: https://www.nichepursuits.com/alright-google-you-win-ill-never-use-private-blog-networks-again/
- Accessed: 2026-08-27
- Source type: discussion (first-person practitioner case study on an industry blog)
- Credibility (1-5): 4 (a named, identifiable practitioner — Spencer Haws — reporting his own direct, quantified financial outcome, with a built-in natural control group (his non-PBN sites were unaffected); high credibility as a documented first-hand case study, though it is a single operator's anecdote, not a statistical sample)
- Recency: published September 22, 2014 — dated, but this is the canonical, frequently-cited first-hand account of Google's well-known September 2014 PBN manual-action sweep, still referenced by current industry writing on PBN risk
- Bias notes: Self-reported by the affected site owner within days of the event — he had some incentive to publicize a dramatic cautionary tale (it drove traffic/credibility to his own blog), but the specific, checkable detail (exact manual-action type, revenue figures, a controlled before/after comparison against his non-PBN sites) makes the account substantive rather than vague fear-mongering.

## Verbatim quotes

> "Google has started penalizing sites that had links from private blog networks (PBNs)."

> "Several of my niche sites have now dropped by as much as 80 to 90% in traffic over the weekend."

> "I had about 10 or so sites that were penalized. This will equate to about $5,000 a month in lost income each month."

> "Google appears to have updated their algorithm to detect PBNs and then is penalizing those sites receiving links from those PBNs."

> "Five of those sites received the 'thin content' manual action. All five of those sites used private blog networks. The remaining 4 sites...did NOT receive any penalty...None of these 4 sites used PBN links."

> "I'm done with PBNs…forever!"

## Relevance

A documented, quantified, first-hand case study of PBN-linked sites losing 80-90% of traffic and roughly $5,000/month in income overnight, as part of Google's known September 2014 PBN manual-action wave — with the operator's own controlled comparison (PBN-linked sites penalized, non-PBN sites in the same portfolio unaffected) isolating PBN links as the causal factor. This is concrete evidence for H3 that the platform risk is not abstract: it has directly destroyed real revenue-generating assets for real operators, which matters for how a PBN-vetting tool's promise ("safe, vetted aged domains") would need to be caveated to buyers.
