## Relevance

This is Google's own confirmed record that it has shipped a spam-related ranking update roughly two to three times a year, continuously, from at least late 2022 through August 2026 (the month this research was conducted) — including a named "link spam update" in December 2022. This directly supports H3's "shrinking market / improving detection" premise: enforcement against link manipulation is not a one-time historical event (e.g., "the 2014 PBN penalty wave") that buyers can treat as settled risk — it is an ongoing, recurring cadence of algorithmic tightening that any PBN-sourcing tool would need to keep pace with indefinitely.
