## Relevance

ADVERSARIAL (H3) and demand-signal (H1). Among 500 self-identified SEO professionals, the named "best-performing tactic" categories are digital PR, HARO, guest posts, and link insertions — PBNs do not appear as a distinct top-line category in the effectiveness breakdown at all, despite link-building budgets broadly growing (58% increased spend). This is consistent with the adversarial hypothesis that buyer budgets are concentrating in non-PBN tactics even as the overall link-building market expands, meaning a PBN-specific tool's addressable market may not be growing in step with the category it serves — though the vendor's conflict of interest (it sells digital PR services) means this reading should not be taken as neutral confirmation.
