## Relevance

ADVERSARIAL on two fronts. First, it complicates H3's "detection is improving" narrative from the opposite direction of sources 25/26/28: an on-record practitioner claims Google "never grows wise" to expired-domain abuse even after "many years," and Google's own Search Liaison gives a hedged, non-committal response rather than a confident claim of reliable detection — suggesting the cat-and-mouse dynamic is genuinely unresolved rather than trending cleanly toward Google winning. Second, and independently useful: it shows expired-domain SEO abuse has been documented in mainstream tech press since at least 2022, meaning the opportunity the founder is evaluating is old and well-trodden, not a fresh gap nobody has tried to fill — raising the question of why no dominant tool has already won this space in four-plus years.
