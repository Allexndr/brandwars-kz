## Relevance

Directly confirms the distinction the research brief asked to pin down: the underlying transaction (registering/buying/reselling an expired domain once it's fully released) is generally legal, with risk concentrated in trademark infringement and cybersquatting (governed by law, e.g., ACPA-type frameworks) — a completely separate question from whether *using* that domain to build an artificial link network violates Google's platform terms. This source only substantiates the legal side of that split; sources 17 and 19-20 substantiate the platform-policy side. Together they support H3's framing precisely: buying/reselling aged domains is not illegal, but building a PBN with them is a platform-policy violation, not a legal one — two distinct and separately-sized risk categories that a product's risk disclosure would need to keep separate.
