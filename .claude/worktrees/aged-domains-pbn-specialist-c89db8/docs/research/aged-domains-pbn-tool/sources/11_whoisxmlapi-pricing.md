## Relevance

Plain current-WHOIS lookups are cheap at indie scale: $30/month for 2,000 queries (~$0.015/query) comfortably covers "hundreds of candidate domains monthly." But the job's actual red-flag signal — ownership-churn history — requires the separate WHOIS History product, priced in an opaque "DRS credit" currency (50 credits/request) whose dollar conversion is not published on any page this agent could access, mirroring the same pricing-opacity problem found with Ahrefs. Net for H2: the cheap/easy WHOIS data (current snapshot) is a non-issue for cost feasibility, but the actually-valuable historical data needed to replicate the specialist's judgment is priced behind an undisclosed credit rate, so a solo builder cannot fully cost this leg out without opening a paid account.
