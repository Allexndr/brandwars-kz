## Relevance

ADVERSARIAL (H3). Direct case-study evidence that Google's August 2025 spam update specifically identified and devalued PBN-style link networks — including links that were up to 5 years old and had been "working" undetected for years before being caught and unwound. This matters beyond simple "PBNs get penalized": it shows detection is retroactive and can claw back value from aged, previously-effective link networks long after acquisition, meaning even a domain that passes vetting today carries an ongoing, unpriced tail risk that no point-in-time vetting (human or automated) can fully rule out — directly undercutting confidence in a "vetted = safe" product promise.
