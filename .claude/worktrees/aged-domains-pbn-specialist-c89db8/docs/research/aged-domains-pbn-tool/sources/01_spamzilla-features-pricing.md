## Relevance

SpamZilla already automates a numeric "cleanliness" score (1–100) derived from backlink/history/anchor-text signals, and both an independent reviewer and a competitor's blog describe it as the category's spam-detection leader — so a new entrant cannot claim spam-scoring itself as a novel feature. Critically for **H1**, the same review that praises the score explicitly states it is "a strong signal but not a substitute for manual review on high-value acquisitions" — third-party confirmation, independent of the freelance-gig evidence, that even the most spam-focused existing tool is positioned (by reviewers, not just skeptical buyers) as a triage aid rather than a trust-it-and-buy final answer. Pricing ($37/mo standalone) sets a reference point for what buyers already pay for automated-only vetting, against which any new product's price and the freelance day-rate evidence (sources 06–07) can be compared.
